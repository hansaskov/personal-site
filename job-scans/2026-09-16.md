## Research
Envira (formerly AgroRisk) is a Copenhagen-based climate-tech startup founded in 2023. It has 8 employees and is growing fast. The company turns Earth observation data and AI analytics into finance-ready intelligence about climate and environmental risk. Its two live products are Envira Flood (address-level flood risk) and Envira Agro (agricultural risk). Customers include insurers, banks, agribusinesses, and public agencies.

CEO Theodor Christensen founded Envira after roles at the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority (Deputy Director and Head of Sustainability and Climate) and Danske Bank (sustainability compliance). He was previously a Visiting Scholar at Columbia University. This regulatory and financial background shapes the company's focus on auditability and integrating climate risk into existing financial workflows (CRR/CRD, SFDR, CSRD, EU Taxonomy).

Stack and engineering culture:
- The platform is built in Python and React on a geospatial database, running in the cloud.
- Internal tooling includes a desktop application with a Rust core.
- AI-assisted development is a hard requirement and the basis of all engineering work. They expect candidates to direct AI tools precisely, review output critically, and stay fully accountable for what ships.
- The team values breadth, judgment, and simplicity over microservices and resume-driven development.
- Hiring process: short screening call, a two-hour practical test (AI tools expected), second round with the team.

Awards and backing:
- Winner of the EU Big Data from Space Award 2025 (ESA Φ-lab).
- Finalist for the EARSC European Start-Up Award 2026.
- Backed by NextGenerationEU and Danish innovation funding.

Hiring contact: Theodor Christensen, CEO & Founder (theodor@envira.dk).

Why Hans fits:
- He has built, deployed, and operated a production platform end-to-end (TriVision PI V4, 420M+ inspections/year), which matches Envira's "tell us about something you've built and run yourself" hiring hook.
- He has hands-on experience with Python, TypeScript/React, SQL, Docker, AWS, CI/CD, and Linux, all listed in the posting.
- He uses agentic AI tools daily and understands that the judgment is on the engineer, not the model.
- He is comfortable being the person who debugs production and owns the cloud environment.

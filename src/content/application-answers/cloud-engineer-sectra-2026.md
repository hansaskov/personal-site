## Please tell us what made you apply for this position and in what way it matches your interests and future ambitions.

Sectra builds IT that helps clinicians diagnose and treat patients, especially in cancer care. When a radiologist opens a patient's images, the system has to work right away. In September, Region Østdanmark chose to extend Sectra's cloud solution to all its hospitals, and Sectra runs the whole solution. That makes Cloud Operations a direct part of patient care, and that is the work I want to do.

The role matches my interests well. I enjoy running systems in production and making them simpler, more stable and faster. I also like automating manual work, so a task is done the same way every time. My master's thesis was about monitoring systems even when parts of them fail. My ambition is to specialise in running critical cloud systems. I want to become an engineer that colleagues and customers can rely on when something goes wrong.

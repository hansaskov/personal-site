## What about Trackunit caught your interest?

Trackunit's purpose of eliminating downtime in construction. Construction is one of the largest industries in the world, and every machine that stands still costs time and money. With more than 2 million connected assets, Trackunit already holds data that can change how the industry works. The Integrations effort brings in the data that still lives in customers' ERPs and other systems, so the picture becomes complete. Building that foundation directly supports your goal of uniting the industry through useful data. I also like that the team works close to its customers and ships in small, frequent releases.

## How can your leader be the best leader for you?

By being a sparring partner. When there are hard priorities or technical trade-offs, I value thinking them through together and reaching a clear decision. In a new team that builds integrations from scratch, many early choices will shape the architecture for years. A leader who is available for those discussions and backs the team towards stakeholders and partners gives us the room to make those choices well.

## What is the most helpful feedback you've received, and how did you use it?

The most helpful feedback I have received is to break large tasks into smaller deliveries. Earlier I could spend a long time on a big piece of work before others saw it. Today I split my work into small steps that can be shipped and tested one at a time. Each step gets its own tests, so I know it works before I build the next one. Colleagues and customers can react sooner, and problems show up while they are still small and easy to fix. It also fits well with how Trackunit favours small, frequent releases.

My name is Hans Askov, i am a software engineer who studied at SDU and i find Evosep a really exciting company with lots of potential! I previously worked at TriVision, a robotic company in Odense, where i primaraly programmed using C++ for the robotics vision system and TypeScript for web dev. During this time i ended up taking ownership of the data ingestion pipeline, where i implemented an updated version that allowed the company to scale their system further and to more customers. 

I am a strong advocate for test-first development. Before writing a new feature or fixing a bug, i start by writing a test that describes the expected behaviour and watch it fail. This forces me to think through the requirements and edge cases up front, and the result is code with a clear purpose and an API that is easy to use. Over time the tests become a safety net that lets the team refactor and ship with confidence, instead of fearing that a change will quietly break something elsewhere. For software that controls instruments and handles valuable samples, as at Evosep, i believe this kind of discipline is essential. 

I am very passionate and eager to work with software development and thrive working with a close team. I live in Odense and can start right away.
